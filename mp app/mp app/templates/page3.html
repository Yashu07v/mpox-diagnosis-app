Mpox Prevention Guide:

How to Protect Yourself

  • Avoid close contact with individuals showing symptoms.
  • Regularly wash your hands with soap and water.
  • Disinfect frequently touched surfaces daily.
  • Wear protective masks in crowded places.
  • Avoid sharing personal items.

Boost Your Immunity

  • Maintain a balanced diet rich in vitamins.
  • Exercise regularly to stay healthy.
  • Get adequate sleep to strengthen your immune system.
  • Stay hydrated throughout the day.
